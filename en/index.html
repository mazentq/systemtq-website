Sales Invoice

A lightning-fast sales invoice

Scan the barcode and press Enter — the item is added instantly with its price and unit, and the total and expected profit update before your eyes.

  • Scale barcodes read the weight and price straight from the label.
  • Split payment: cash, bank, e-wallets or customer credit — in one invoice.
  • Hold and recall invoices, and prepare delivery orders with areas, fees and drivers.
  • Item or invoice discounts, automatic offers, and wholesale/retail switching by cashier permission.
  • Full keyboard shortcuts: F12 pay & print, F2 new invoice, F10 hold.
POS

POS for touch screens

Coloured categories and large item tiles — the cashier sells with a tap, no keyboard or mouse.

  • A colour and order for each category; items take their category's colour or their own.
  • Full styling of tile sizes, fonts and what they show (price, quantity) with a live preview.
  • Touch keyboard and number pad appear automatically in input fields.
  • Quick add for favourite items, and a customer display on a second screen showing the invoice live.
Item management

Unlimited item catalogue

Thousands of items appear instantly, with search by name, code or barcode, and editing from the same screen.

  • Multiple units (piece, box, carton), each with its own price, barcode and conversion factor.
  • Colours and flavours each with its own barcode and stock.
  • Main and sub categories, expiry tracking, non-stock service items, and automatic codes and barcodes.
  • Excel import and quick bulk price editing.
Stock overview

Live stock — know its value at any moment

Every item's balance in every warehouse, with its cost, sale price, total value and your expected profit.

  • Weighted average cost calculated automatically with every purchase.
  • Barcode stocktake with the quantity difference and its value live, plus adjustments and damage with reasons.
  • Transfers between warehouses, item card, and low-stock, expiry and reorder alerts.
Treasury — current balances

Every pound in its place

Cash boxes, banks and e-wallets (Vodafone Cash, InstaPay…) with live balances and a statement for each account.

  • A cash drawer per shift in the cashier's name, handed to the treasury automatically at close.
  • Receipt and payment vouchers, account transfers, expenses by type, and wallet transfer fees.
  • Block or allow overdrafts per user.
Home — performance and charts

Decisions based on numbers

Today's, this week's and this month's statistics, top sellers, sales by cashier, and profit & loss.

  • More than 30 reports: sales, purchases, returns, stock, slow movers, damage, shifts, tax, statements.
  • Every report: search, column filters, sorting, totals, Excel export, and A4 or PDF printing.
  • Manager alerts on WhatsApp when shifts open and close and invoices are edited.

Shifts

A cash drawer per shift… no room for unexplained shortages

Opening a shift creates a temporary drawer in the cashier's name and shift number. All the shift's cash goes into it; at close the cashier counts the drawer, any shortage or overage shows at once, and all the cash is handed over automatically to the receiving treasury.

  • A detailed closing receipt: money in, money out, accounts and cash reconciliation.
  • Closing needs the password of whoever opened the shift, and figures can be hidden from the cashier.
  • A shift can't close before delivery drivers are settled.

FAQ

Everything you want to know before starting

Is the program available in English?
Yes. From version 1.1 you choose the program's language (Arabic or English) per PC in Settings or on the sign-in screen, and you choose the language of the printed invoice and the shift-closing report separately from the program's language.
What happens if the server PC stops while selling?
A connection notice appears and the cashier keeps making cash sales from the copy of items and prices saved on their PC, even after restarting the program. When the connection returns the invoices are posted to the server automatically, exactly once, and any doubtful invoice is shown to the manager for review.
Does it work without internet?
Yes, completely. Sales, stock, accounts and reports all run on the shop's local network. The internet is only used for updates, optional cloud sync and WhatsApp messages. Even if the server PC itself stops, the cashier keeps selling from their PC and the invoices are posted automatically when the connection returns.
How many cashier PCs can I run?
One server PC and any number of cashier PCs on the same network. Each cashier has their own shift and drawer, and all PCs see the same items and balances live. Each PC is activated with its own license.
Is my data safe?
Your data stays on your own PC in a SQL Server database. The program takes a backup automatically at every sign-out into the folder you choose, you can restore with one click, and there's optional sync to a cloud copy.
I have items and customers in Excel — do I start from zero?
No. You can import items (with categories, units, prices and barcodes), customers and suppliers straight from an Excel file, with a ready template you download from inside the program.
How do I activate after the trial?
The «Activation» screen shows your PC code — send it with the WhatsApp button, then paste the license key you receive and press «Activate». Your trial data stays as it is. With yearly licenses the program reminds you to renew 30 days ahead.
How do updates reach me?
The update address is stored inside your license, so there's nothing to set up. With automatic updates the program downloads a new version in the background and installs it when you close it, or you install it right away from «Update available». Updates are digitally signed, so nobody can push a fake version.
Does it suit my business?
It's built mainly for supermarkets, groceries and hypermarkets, and suits clothing and accessories shops (colours and sizes), bookshops, retail pharmacies, sweets and bakery shops (manufacturing module), and spice shops (scale barcodes).
Is there technical support?
Yes — direct contact with the developer on WhatsApp for installation, training and questions, and a full user guide inside the program itself.
